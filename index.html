Stride Privacy Policy

Stride is made by Xanchor Capital Technologies. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Stride is a calendar, reminders and planning app for Android. This page explains what information Stride uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: Stride has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your plans are stored on your phone.

Information stored on your phone

Everything you create in Stride stays on your device: events, to-dos, grocery lists, routines (such as water, medicine and bill reminders), reminder settings, your chosen theme and the name you give the app. We do not have a copy of it and we cannot see it.

Information that leaves your phone

Some features need to send a small amount of information to a service so they can work. This only happens when you use that feature.

FeatureWhat is sentSent toWhy
AI day planning (“Plan my day”, when it uses AI)The text you type or say, the date you are planning, and the titles and times of events already on that dayGoogle, through Firebase AI Logic (Gemini)To turn your sentence into a list of events and avoid clashes. Simple sentences are understood on your phone without sending anything.
Voice inputYour speech, while you hold the microphoneYour phone’s speech recognition service (usually Google)To turn speech into text. Stride does not record or keep audio.
Weather on HomeYour approximate location (latitude and longitude)Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com)To show the local weather and air quality. Stride does not keep your location.
App protectionA token that shows the request comes from the real Stride appGoogle (Firebase App Check, Play Integrity)To stop other apps from misusing Stride’s AI features.

These providers handle the information under their own privacy policies: Google and Open-Meteo.

Your other calendars

If you allow it, Stride reads the calendars already on your phone (for example Google Calendar) so their events, such as meetings and travel tickets, appear in Stride. Stride only reads them. It does not change them, and it does not upload them anywhere.

Permissions Stride asks for

  • Notifications, alarms and full-screen notifications: to show reminders at the right time, including on the lock screen.
  • Battery (run in background): so the phone does not stop reminders from arriving.
  • Microphone: only when you choose to plan by speaking.
  • Location: only for the weather on Home.
  • Calendar: only to show events from your phone’s other calendars.

Every permission is optional. You can turn any of them off in your phone’s Settings, and Stride will keep working without that feature.

Backups

Stride can save a backup file of your data. You choose where the file is saved, and Stride does not send it anywhere.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don’t sell or share your information.
  • We don’t require an account or sign-in.

Deleting your information

You can delete any item inside Stride. To remove everything, clear Stride’s storage in your phone’s Settings or uninstall the app. Because your data is kept on your phone and not on our servers, there is nothing else for us to delete.

Children

Stride is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Information sent to the services above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). You can also protect Stride with an app lock on your phone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app.
